Holiday BLOG

8.15 p.m. @zeti1223

Hi, everyone! We landed in Tenerife on Saturday afternoon. The flight was so long, I thought it would never end! I watched three movies and still had time to spare. My legs were so cramped! The landing was super smooth!

The weather here is absolutely perfect. It's sunny and warm, not too hot. We've already done so much!

We've already been up Mount Teide, the volcano! We took a cable car up most of the way. The views were incredible – it felt like we were on another planet! We've also spent a day at the beach. I went in the ocean for the first time ever! The water was much colder than I expected, but it was amazing. I loved the waves!

Yesterday we visited the Los Gigantes cliffs. They were massive! The cliffs are so huge and just drop straight into the sea. We walked around the little marina in the town and had some ice cream.
